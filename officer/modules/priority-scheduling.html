How Priority Scores are Calculated (Transparent Algorithm)

Scores range from 0 to 100 based strictly on biological and operational vulnerability. Land size is intentionally NOT a positive factor, preventing large commercial farms from monopolizing harvesters over women smallholders.

Pillar 1: Crop Spoilage 35% Weight Pre-monsoon risk & grain over-ripeness
Pillar 2: Window Cutoff 25% Weight Days past optimal cutting maturity
Pillar 3: Postponements 25% Weight Compensates farmers bumped previously
Pillar 4: Waiting Days 15% Weight Queue duration without machine dispatch
